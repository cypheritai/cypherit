Hey everyone 👋

I kept running into the same problem: I'd find a YouTube tutorial to fix something, and then spend 20 minutes watching someone talk before getting to the 3 steps I actually needed.

So I built CypherIt.

**How it works:**

2. AI extracts the key steps
3. Get actionable instructions in ~60 seconds

**What you get:**
- Step-by-step instructions
- Why each step matters
- How to verify it worked
- Quick summary
