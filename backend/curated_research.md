# Curated Video Research for CypherIt Discovery

## How to Find Videos
For each category, search YouTube with these queries and pick the best results:
- Look for videos with **closed captions (CC)**
- Prefer **1M+ views** = proven quality
- Check **like/dislike ratio**
- Verify transcript works (test extraction)

## Video ID Format

Once you find a good video, grab the ID from the URL:
- `https://youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID_HERE`
- Example: `https://youtube.com/watch?v=6_pru8U2RmM` → ID is `6_pru8U2RmM`
